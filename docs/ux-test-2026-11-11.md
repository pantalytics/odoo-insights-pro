# User test 11-11-2026 and the 2.1 decisions

Five user stories (issue #18), tested by four fresh "human" testers in the
browser (no code, demo project) and one agent test on production data.

| Story | Result | Main reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Director: on schedule at a glance | FAIL | Map hard to find (6 clicks, 2 dead ends); flows hidden in a dropdown; no dates |
| 2 Project lead: flow to task on iPad | FAIL | 6 taps (target 3); Map only in a hover menu; swipe zooms instead of scrolls; no owner on chips |
| 3 Consultant: progress updates the map | PASS, barely | Progress on a 4th tab; stage and progress disagree |
| 4 Trainer: who still needs training | FAIL | No names per role on the map; training mode hidden |
| 5 Agent: open in flow X and who | FAIL | Map data held only placed tasks; a flow showed nothing |

Common thread: too much on screen (zoom slider, app chips, count bubbles,
split bars, icons, two colour modes, external labels) and too little of what
people came for (which flow is behind, who must still be trained).

## Decisions for 2.1 (binding)

1. **One place to start.** Projects menu "Map" opens the map. With one
   mapped project it opens that one; with more, a plain list of projects.
   The project form keeps its "Map" button. Phases, Roles and ADKAR scores
   move to Projects > Configuration. One name everywhere: "Map".
2. **Flows first.** The map opens with a flow list on the left, worst first
   (lowest Tested %, then latest deadline passed): name, a thin bar, Tested %,
   deadline, and "Late" when the deadline has passed below 100%. No
   dropdown. Clicking a flow highlights its tasks and lists its open tasks
   with their owner, also the tasks without a place.
3. **One plain grid, no zoom.** Phases as columns, roles as rows, task chips
   in the cells: a coloured dot and the name. Normal scrolling. Removed: the
   zoom slider, wheel and pinch zoom, the Company and Detail stops, the Odoo
   app chip row, count bubbles, split bars, icons, the "External" label.
   Rows without tasks are hidden. Tap targets at least 44 px; row and column labels never cut off (wrap). Click a chip: a small panel (flow, role,
   phase, progress word, owners, hours, Open task).
4. **Progress is the stage.** Each task stage carries a progress step
   (Open, Decided, Built, Tested, Trained, Live). The task's progress follows
   its stage, so the kanban and the map always agree, and there is no extra
   field to set. Loading the template gives the project these six stages.
   Legend: three colours, grey Open, amber Decided or Built, green Tested or
   further; the panel shows the exact word.
5. **Training per person, in one place.** No "By training" colour mode.
   Click a role name: its people, and per person nothing to infer: "To
   train on" lists the role's tasks below Trained; an ADKAR band only when
   three people are scored. A "Training" button above the grid opens a
   simple list: role, person, tasks still to train.
6. **Task form.** Role and phase sit above the fold next to the tags, labelled
   "Role" and "Phase". No Map tab. A flow (parent task) shows its Tested %;
   role and phase are hidden there.
7. **Agent contract.** `get_blueprint_map_data` returns every non-flow task
   of the project (role and phase may be false) with owner names, stage and
   progress; a flow lists all its subtasks. The map places only tasks with a
   role and a phase. `import_blueprint` works over RPC for managers (#24).

## Hard calls made while building 2.1

- Progress moved to the stage, so the upgrade drops the old per-task
  progress: every task starts at Open. Production held it only on demo and
  template data.
- The phase's Odoo apps are removed (field and column), not just hidden.
- The training list (decision 5) is the project's tasks with a role below
  Trained, grouped by role, with the role's people in one column; no
  separate model.
- A blueprint import gives a project the six progress stages too, not only
  the template, so a project filled over RPC has progress as well.

## Retest round 2 (2.1 build) and the 2.2 fixes

| Story | Result | Remaining |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Director | Marginal pass | Map in 1 click, answer in ~10 s; three flows tie at 0% Late, date unlabelled |
| 2 Project lead | PASS | 4 taps; selected flow's tasks below the fold, no hours in the list |
| 3 Consultant | FAIL | Map follows the task, but the flow cards on the kanban stay "Open" and "0/4" |
| 4 Trainer | FAIL, narrow | "Training" list never says "still to train"; Trained looks like Tested |
| 5 Agent | PASS | One call names the worst flow, its open tasks, stage and owner |

Fixes (binding):

1. A flow's stage follows its tasks: the lowest step among its non-cancelled
   subtasks (only in projects with a map). The kanban and the map agree.
   The flow card on the kanban shows "Tested NN%".
2. Clicking a chip anywhere (grid or list) opens the task panel; "Open task"
   leads to the form, where the stage bar moves it forward.
3. Flow list: a summary line on top ("3 of 10 flows late"), each flow shows
   "Due 6 Nov", "Late · 5 days" and "N open". Ties break on more open tasks.
4. The selected flow's open tasks show directly under it, scrolled into view,
   each with owner and "spent of planned h".
5. Four colours, plain words: grey Not started (Open), amber In progress
   (Decided, Built), green Tested, dark green Trained (Trained, Live). The
   panel shows the stage once.
6. "Training" becomes "Still to train (N)". Its list is titled "Still to
   train", grouped by role and expanded, internal roles only, people names
   wrapped, with a visible filter "Still to train".
7. The grid shows a fade at the right edge when it scrolls sideways.

## 2.3: answers on top, flow lines back (owner, 11-11)

Owner feedback after go-live: the Google Maps idea and the lines of Wytse's
swimlane charts are missing. A first plan brought three zoom levels back
(Company blocks, Tasks, Flow) with + and - buttons, pinch and Ctrl + wheel.
The owner then narrowed it: zoom is not required. What he wants to see at
once: where the project stands and whether it is on schedule, what still has
to happen, how each flow goes, whether everyone is trained, and whether the
choices are recorded. And he wants Odoo's own search bar on the map.
Decisions (binding, they replace the zoom plan):

1. **The map is an Odoo view.** "Map" is a view type on project tasks, in a
   project's view switcher next to Kanban and List, so search, filters,
   group by and favourites come from Odoo. Projects > Map stays the shortcut
   (one mapped project opens directly, more give a plain list); old map
   links still work. New filters: Activity, No place on the map, Not
   decided, Still to do (next to Still to train); group by Role and Phase.
2. **Five plain answers on top**, over the tasks of the current search, each
   a way into the detail that already exists:
   - On schedule?: the next milestone, its date and "On track" or
     "Late · N days", and "N of M flows late". Tap: the milestones.
   - Still to do: open tasks (below Tested) and how many have no owner.
     Tap: the filter Still to do.
   - Flows: "N of M flows tested". Tap: the worst flow.
   - Training: "N of M tasks trained" (internal roles) and the people still
     to train. Tap: the Still to train list.
   - Choices: "N of M tasks decided". Tap: the filter Not decided.
3. **Flow lines.** Clicking a flow shows it as a swimlane, like Wytse's
   charts: a column per step in task order, left to right; only the roles
   it uses as rows; each task in its role row at its step; the phase as a
   small label above the steps (adjacent steps of one phase share it).
   Arrows join the steps left to right: straight within a role, an elbow
   when the role changes. A breadcrumb "Map > S04 ..."
   goes back; so does tapping the flow again.
4. **Kept as they are**: the flow list (worst first, summary, due, late,
   open), the grid of chips, the chip and role panels, the tasks without a
   place, the legend with stage words, 44 px touch targets, no page-wide
   sideways scroll on the iPad, plain wheel and swipe scroll.
5. Not built: zoom levels, + and - buttons, pinch and Ctrl + wheel zoom,
   Company blocks. Retest the five stories; all must still pass.

## Round 4 (2.3 build): four fresh testers

| Story | Result | Remaining |
|---|---|---|

Fixes (binding):

1. One verdict on the schedule card: "Late · N days" (red) when the next
   milestone's date has passed unreached; else "At risk" (amber) when any
   flow is late, with "N flows late"; "On track" only when neither.
2. The progress step is a search field ("Progress step"), so typing "test"
   offers "Search Progress step for: test". The filter "Not tested" is now
   "Still to do", the name of the card that applies it.
3. The five cards look tappable: pointer, hover and focus state, a small
   chevron; a card whose filter is on shows as active.
4. Under any search, role rows and phase columns without a matching task
   are hidden.
5. The sticky role column stays inside the grid area; it no longer slides
   over the overview cards.
6. Hours read "0:00 spent of 5:00 planned" in the list and the panel.
7. The selected flow lists all its steps in order, the tested ones muted
   with their stage word, as in its chart. Back from a task form, the flow
   stays selected and in view.
8. The task panel docks at the right of the grid area and narrows the
   chart instead of covering it (an overlay on phones); its close button is
   44 px; the description is plain text.
9. The task panel has "Move to <next stage>" next to "Open task": the
   project's next stage by sequence with a higher step, written with the
   user's rights; the map refreshes after.

## Round 5 (owner on real data): the map as a map

On the real project (115 tasks, 9 phases, 8 roles, long Dutch titles) the
owner did not know where to look: the page scrolled far down. Decisions
(binding, 2.3.1):

1. **It is a map with levels of detail**, like a vector map: one stage with
   a continuous zoom from 0 to 4, and every element declares the zoom range
   it appears in (`LAYERS` in `map_view.js`, like minzoom/maxzoom):
   - z0 Company: the first sentence of the company page, keywords, from ->
     to (systems), the timeline from start to target with the milestones
     and today.
   - z1 Project: the timeline and one bar for all tasks of the search,
     stacked by step (Open to Live, grey to dark green), with the next
     milestone and one verdict (On track, At risk, Late).
   - z2 Flows: every flow as one bar by step, a one-line summary, up to three
     keywords, open, due and late. Worst first: lowest mean step, then late,
     then more open.
   - z3 Flow: its swimlane (a column per step, its roles as rows, arrows in
     task order) and its steps with owner and hours.
   - z4 Task: owner, hours, stage, the first line, "Move to <next stage>".
2. **Zoom like a map**: + and - (44 px), pinch on touch, Ctrl/Cmd + wheel; a
   plain wheel or swipe scrolls and never zooms. Clicking an element flies
   to it. The breadcrumb (Company > Project > Flows > S04 > task) zooms back
   out. The zoom and focus are kept per project for the browser tab.
3. **One focal point, nothing that competes**: the answer cards, the left
   flow list beside the grid, the roles x phases grid and the four-colour
   legend are gone. "Still to do", "Not decided" and "Still to train" are
   search filters; the Still to train list opens from a role's panel.
4. **The page never scrolls**; the stage and the swimlane scroll inside
   their own box. Chip names wrap to two lines, the full name in the title.
5. New fields: the company page, keywords and systems on the project (its
   start and target dates are the standard ones); a summary and keywords on
   flows and tasks.
